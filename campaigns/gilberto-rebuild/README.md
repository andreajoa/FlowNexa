# Gilberto — English conversation and seasonal book campaign

Two independently editable flows are generated by `flows.js`:

- **Direct**: every new inbound Direct/story message, a fresh routing decision for each message. Up to 29 nodes. Concurrent messages wait durably for the previous reply instead of disappearing into its cursor.
- **Comments**: a first private response to a new top-level comment on any post (23 nodes), limited to once per contact for this flow. The person must reply in Direct before the Direct flow continues. No unsolicited reminders.

Initial questions route before a greeting. Priorities: immediate safety, contact requests, refusals and acknowledgements, explicit campaign requests, then author credibility, existing-order support, paperback, book enquiries, anger, sleeplessness, identity, faith, separation and language. Unknown requests get a transparent automated menu and a request for clarification. This is deterministic English phrase matching with editable expressions, not a generative AI or reliable universal crisis detector. No contact is diagnosed; crisis copy does not sell the book. STOP persists across the account's campaigns. No medical history is collected.

## Seasonal rotation (UTC, inclusive)

Only when someone asks about the book, and after safety/refusal/support routing:

| Dates | Creative |
|---|---|
| 18 October–30 November 2026 | Black Friday |
| 1–25 December 2026 | Christmas |
| 26 December 2026–7 January 2027 | New Year |
| Outside those dates | Evergreen book explanation |

These are configured editorial windows, not sale deadlines. No discount, coupon, guaranteed outcome or fixed price is advertised. An explicit BLACKFRIDAY, CHRISTMAS or NEWYEAR request can open the corresponding material outside its default window. Price and purchase terms remain on the official storefront. Campaign artwork is delivered in Direct with a real clickable purchase button; a private comment reply contains text and the official link only.

## Creative files and suggested captions

Files in `public/campaigns/gilberto-rebuild/`, generated with the author's existing English book image:

- `black-friday.png`: **A new chapter starts with you.** Caption: “A personal story of rebuilding after heartbreak. Discover Gilberto de Souza's book this Black Friday. DM **BLACKFRIDAY** for the English book page and edition options.”
- `christmas.png`: **Give hope. Begin a new chapter.** Caption: “A story of faith, heartbreak and rebuilding. Explore a thoughtful reading choice this Christmas. DM **CHRISTMAS** to discover the book.”
- `new-year.png`: **Your story is still being written.** Caption: “A new year can be a moment to reflect on the next chapter. Discover Gilberto's personal account of rebuilding after betrayal. DM **NEWYEAR** for the English book page.”

These captions and artwork are prepared for posting; this implementation does not publish Instagram posts or bulk-message existing followers. Each new eligible inbound message or comment may trigger the published flows. Publication likes, saves and shares between third parties are not supported DM triggers. A share sent to Gilberto as an incoming Direct attachment can enter the Direct flow; no claim is made that all share formats produce an event.

## Content sources and limits

Read the supplied 114-page English manuscript, official website `/en`, official link hub `/links`, current profile and 25 recent captions. The manuscript is not included in the repository. Chapters support references to identity, anger, purpose, faith and hope. Do not repeat manuscript passages discouraging therapy or medication. The author is presented as sharing lived experience; no clinical credentials, testimonials or sales numbers are invented.

- [Official English storefront](https://www.gilberto-souza.com/en) — its visible prices conflict; deliberately do not hardcode a price.
